Privacy Architecture

Designed from the Ground Up for Privacy

GlassChat doesn't bolt on privacy as an afterthought. Every system is architected from day one to leave no trace, store no data, and expose no metadata.

  • 🗄️

    No Image Database

    Photos are stored only in your private Supabase buckets with strict user-level access controls. Nobody except the two chat participants can ever access your media.

  • 👻

    True Vanish Mode

    Not just "hidden" — physically destroyed. When you exit a vanish session, the app purges all local Room DB records, clears Firestore documents, and wipes Supabase media simultaneously.

  • 📷

    Screenshot Detection + Block

    Dual-layer protection: hardware WindowManager SECURE flags prevent screen capture, while a filesystem watcher detects screenshots and alerts you instantly in the chat timeline.

  • 🔗

    Connection-Only Messaging

    Your chat list is never exposed publicly. Only users you've mutually accepted as connections can message you — eliminating spam, harassment, and unwanted contact entirely.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know, answered plainly.

Is GlassChat completely free?

Yes — completely free, no ads, no subscriptions, no hidden costs. Download the APK and you have full access to every single feature immediately.

How does Vanish Mode actually work?

When you close a Vanish Mode session, GlassChat simultaneously purges all Room database records, deletes Firestore conversation documents, and removes any uploaded media from Supabase buckets — on both devices at once.

Can anyone screenshot my chats?

No. GlassChat applies WindowManager SECURE flags to all chat screens. Any screenshot attempt produces a blank black image. Additionally, a file-system watcher detects attempts and alerts you in the chat immediately.

Are video and voice calls private?

Completely. Calls use WebRTC — a direct peer-to-peer technology where audio/video streams go directly between the two devices. No server ever receives, records, or can intercept your call streams.

How does the alarm bypass DND?

GlassChat uses Android's AlarmManager with PRIORITY_ALARM and a foreground service with the AudioManager STREAM_ALARM channel. This combination is explicitly allowed to bypass DND by Android's OS, identical to how system clock alarms work.

Are my images stored anywhere?

Images are stored in private Supabase buckets, accessible only to the two participants in that conversation. No admin, no third party, and no algorithm can access them. In Vanish Mode, they're deleted entirely.

How does Connection-Only messaging work?

You can only start a conversation with users who've mutually accepted your connection request. Strangers cannot message you, cannot find you unless you share your handle, and cannot see your profile or status.

Does it work offline?

Yes! GlassChat is built offline-first. All messages are written to local Room SQLite storage first, so the UI updates instantly even without internet. When connectivity is restored, everything syncs automatically.
