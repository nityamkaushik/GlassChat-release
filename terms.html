Emergency Alarm Feature

IMPORTANT: Emergency Alarm is NOT a replacement for medical alert systems and is NOT a replacement for 911 or professional emergency services.

  • Reliability depends on network connectivity, device battery, operating-system permissions, notification delivery, and recipient device status.
  • Emergency Alarm may fail if the recipient's device is off, out of battery, offline, muted by system policy, or unable to receive notifications.
  • Bypassing Do Not Disturb requires specific Android permissions and may not work unless the recipient has granted those permissions.
  • Use Emergency Alarm only for genuine emergencies or urgent, time-sensitive safety needs.
  • Users who require medical alerts should use dedicated medical alert systems and local emergency services.
  • Misuse, harassment, spam, or repeated false alarms may result in account suspension or termination.

Vanish Mode Disclaimer

Vanish Mode messages auto-delete after a configured timer, but deletion does not guarantee that a recipient never saw or copied the content.

Vanish Mode does not prevent recipients from photographing the screen with another device.

Vanish Mode may not prevent screenshots on rooted devices, modified Android ROMs, or devices with compromised system protections.

Share sensitive ephemeral content only with verified contacts you trust.

Screenshot Shield Disclaimer

Screenshot Shield uses Android FLAG_SECURE to request OS-level screenshot and screen-recording protection.

FLAG_SECURE may not work on rooted devices, modified Android ROMs, all Android versions, or all screen recording methods.

No app-level screenshot shield can prevent external-camera capture.
